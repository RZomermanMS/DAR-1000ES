The following comparisons are nominal and are not measurements of fitted
parts. The manufacturer PDFs/pages were not directly accessible during this
review; quoted dimensions were obtained from indexed text and must be
checked against the drawings and the exact purchased parts before release.

* ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1: the carrier models two socket rows 22.86 mm apart,
  with 22 pins at 2.54 mm pitch. The indexed Espressif v1.1 drawing gives a
  25.40 x 62.74 mm PCB, not including the module antenna overhang. Its
  centered nominal width would be x=63.73..89.13 mm. The footprints do not
  model the complete installed board, socket height, module or USB plugs.
  https://dl.espressif.com/dl/schematics/PCB_ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1_V1.1_20220429.pdf
  https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.1.html
  Confirm the actual board revision and USB connector: different boards or
  revisions may have different dimensions and mating envelopes.
* Antennas: the provisional rule rectangles x=62..90, y=5..27 and
  y=165..187 mm remove neither the carrier substrate below the antenna nor
  nearby chassis metal. Indexed Espressif guidance recommends placing the
  antenna beyond the base-board edge or cutting away material beneath it,
  and allowing 15 mm clearance inside the housing in all directions.
  Neither rule rectangle demonstrates this. Check the actual module,
  orientation, substrate, enclosure and mounting hardware; RF performance
  must be measured.
  https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/esp-hardware-design-guidelines-en-master-esp32s3.pdf
* Adafruit U5: modeled outline approximately 17.78 x 35.56 mm, versus
  indexed product dimensions 17.7 x 35.5 x 4.6 mm. Socket/header height and
  any STEMMA plug are not included in the footprint.
  https://www.adafruit.com/product/4918
* JST-XH: the modeled four-way header body is 12.4 mm wide; compare with the
  manufacturer's S4B-XH-A drawing. J3–J11 face the left edge, with their
  modeled mating faces about 0.6 mm inside it; J2/J12 face the top, about
  0.5 mm inside. Mating housings, fingers, wires and bend radii extend beyond
  the PCB. Their envelopes and adjacent connector access are not modeled.
  https://www.jst-connector.com/datasheet/jst-connector/S4B-XH-A.pdf
* H1–H6 are 3.4 mm holes with assumed 7.5 mm-diameter courtyards, not
  specified hardware. At H6 (95,200), the centered nominal ESP board edge
  is about 5.87 mm from the hole center, leaving 2.12 mm to the assumed
  head radius. Actual heads, washers, drivers and standoffs are unverified.

Before any manufacture or power-up, obtain and compare the actual
manufacturer drawings, then measure the enclosure, bosses and available
height and trial-fit the selected modules, sockets, plugs, cables and
hardware. DRC and these nominal comparisons cannot certify mechanical fit,
RF operation, antenna clearance, soldering access or electrical safety.
