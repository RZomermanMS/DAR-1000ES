DAR-1000ES v5 SCHEMATIC — NOT FOR FABRICATION
================================================

Experimental synchronized and routed v5 boards are available for inspection;
see INSPECTION.txt. The v3 PCB does not implement these changes. None is
fabrication-ready. Do not fabricate, assemble or connect to a Sony unit until
the PCB is independently electrically and physically reviewed and tested
through all power-up, brownout, shutdown and fault conditions.

Connection and firmware delta from v3
-------------------------------------
J4 pin 1 FLSTB, pin 2 FLSDATA, pin 3 FLSCLK. These are the existing U4
buffered outputs 8, 3 and 11 respectively. Their input GPIOs remain
controller GPIO7, GPIO8 and GPIO5 respectively. The three v3 labels were
detached from their existing wires; the labels now attach to J4 pins.
Verify cable orientation and the externally requested pin-function mapping
against the actual mating equipment before connecting it.

U2 now belongs to AUDIO, not CONTROLLER. Remap firmware as follows:
  Function    AUDIO GPIO (header pin)    U2 input -> output -> connector
  A.MUTE      4 (J1.4)                  9 -> 8 -> J9.2
  D.MUTE      5 (J1.5)                  2 -> 3 -> J9.3
  RELAY       6 (J1.6)                 12 -> 11 -> J9.4
  spare       7 (J1.7)                  5 -> 6 -> no connection
  ENABLE_U2   8 (J1.12)                 U9.9
Controller GPIO6, GPIO9, GPIO10, GPIO11 and GPIO12 no longer drive U2.
No firmware has been modified; existing firmware must be adapted and tested.
Remaining assignments are as in baseline-v3/GPIO-and-interlock-v3.txt.
The inter-ESP UART remains controller GPIO21 (J3.18) -> audio GPIO13
(J1.19), and controller GPIO47 (J3.17) <- audio GPIO14 (J1.20).
Both ESPs' J3.1 GND pins, previously tied only to each other, now join GND.
Neither audio 3V3 output is tied to either controller 3V3 output. Controller
J1.1 still feeds the local keypad breakout's VIN as in v3.

Interlock — nominal logic, not an all-supply-state safety certification
----------------------------------------------------------------------
MCP100-475DI/TO supervisors U10 and U11 have separate push-pull active-low
reset outputs and associated passive networks. BOTH sense the same Sony
rail, J12.2; they are independent outputs, not independent Sony power
sources and not supervision of the separate ESP 5 V rails.

U10.1 -> R12 1k -> CTRL_POWER_GOOD, R13 100k to GND;
U11.1 -> R14 1k -> AUDIO_POWER_GOOD, R15 100k to GND.
C8 and C14 each 100nF bypass SONY_5V_SENSE to GND. All GPIO enable requests
have 100k pulldowns R7–R11. OE outputs have their existing 10k pullups to
LOCAL_5V, on the buffer/NAND output side, not on the ESP GPIO nets.
U8 and U9 NANDs and the AHCT125 buffers are powered by LOCAL_5V.

  Gate          request      second input         output
  U8 4,5 -> 6   ENABLE_U3    CTRL_POWER_GOOD      U3_OE_N
  U8 9,10 -> 8  ENABLE_U4    CTRL_POWER_GOOD      U4_OE_N
  U9 9,10 -> 8  ENABLE_U2    AUDIO_POWER_GOOD     U2_OE_N
  U9 1,2 -> 3   ENABLE_U6    AUDIO_POWER_GOOD     U6_OE_N
  U9 4,5 -> 6   ENABLE_U7    AUDIO_POWER_GOOD     U7_OE_N

For each correctly powered NAND: request/good = 00, 01 or 10 gives OE HIGH
(disabled); 11 gives OE LOW (enabled). Unused gate inputs are grounded and
their outputs unconnected. ENABLE requests are active HIGH; initialize LOW
and keep LOW during reset. MCP100 datasheet 11187F describes push-pull
active-low RESET, guaranteed output down to VDD=1.0 V, and 475 trip range
4.50–4.75 V and reset release delay 150–700 ms. Its pinout agrees with
the KiCad 10 Power_Supervisor MCP100-475D symbol: pin 1 RESET, 2 VDD,
3 VSS. Source: https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/11187F.pdf
The NAND truth table is not proof of fail-disabled behavior when either
logic supply is out of specification or in the presence of component faults.

Verification and reproducibility
--------------------------------
From the repository root, python hardware/pcb-redesign/v5/generate_schematic.py
reconstructs schematic/project/library files from baseline-v3 only. The
netlist is exported from the generated schematic using KiCad 10.0.2:
  docker run --rm --user "$(id -u):$(id -g)" -e HOME=/work \
    -v "$PWD/hardware/pcb-redesign/v5:/work" -w /work kicad/kicad:10.0.2 \
    sh -c 'kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o DAR-1000ES-v5.net.xml DAR-1000ES-v5.kicad_sch && kicad-cli sch erc --format json --units mm -o DAR-1000ES-v5-ERC.json DAR-1000ES-v5.kicad_sch'
  python hardware/pcb-redesign/v5/verify_netlist.py
KiCad may write .kicad_prl and local configuration/cache files; these are
not part of the deliverable. The XML source path records the container
export location as metadata; project/sheet/library paths do not depend on it.
Generator repeatability was checked by comparing SHA-256 of all generated
files before and after a second run. Adafruit README and license are copied
unchanged from baseline-v3.
DAR-1000ES-v5-schematic.pdf is KiCad's three-sheet schematic export, not
1:1 fabrication artwork. All three pages were rendered with Ghostscript
and inspected for page clipping; the dense inherited main sheet is best
read at high zoom. This visual check does not establish electrical safety.

The verifier checks explicit J2–J12 maps, each NAND's actual individual
pins, OE and enable/pulldown nets, both supervisor networks, capacitor rails,
GPIO-to-5V pullup absence, all four 3V3 header pins, and exact two-terminal
UART endpoints. It classifies every reference as controller, audio or shared,
and rejects unknown references and net names. The v3 pin-net partition is
compared after explicitly accounting for changed terminals and new parts.
It also checks safety-critical resistor values, IC symbol identities and
values, and capacitor values and specified MPNs against the exported XML.
Run `python -B hardware/pcb-redesign/v5/test_verify_netlist.py` for focused
in-memory mutation tests: 168 altered/missing/duplicate component or MPN
cases are rejected. The unmodified XML passes. Run the full verifier with
normal Python, not `python -O`, since its connectivity checks use assertions.
These checks do not prove that the schematic or exported XML matches the
physical fitted components.
The actual nets crossing controller/audio classifications are GND, LOCAL_5V,
SONY_5V_SENSE, Net-(U1-CONTROLLER1-IO21), and
Net-(U1-CONTROLLER1-IO47). This is a netlist check, not a physical isolation
or bench test. It does not prove that connector mating pinouts are correct.
For the section audit, C1/C2 accompany U3/U4 (controller), C3/C4/C5
accompany U2/U6/U7 (audio), and C6/C7 accompany U8/U9 respectively.
The C3 assignment follows its U2 decoupling role even though the v3
placement was near the controller region.

KiCad 10.0.2 ERC: 59 outstanding items, not waived or signed off: 47
pin_not_connected, 9 power_pin_not_driven, 2 pin_not_driven, 1
same_local_global_label. See DAR-1000ES-v5-ERC.json for details.
Inherited project settings ignore single_global_label, four_way_junction,
simulation_model_issue and footprint_filter. The netlist checks above are
needed because ERC alone can miss disconnected global labels; they do not
replace a complete ERC review. In particular unconnected dev-board EN/power
abstract pins and inherited pin-not-connected items require explicit review.

Remaining safety risks — unresolved
-----------------------------------
No electrical bench validation has been performed. Verify Sony cable pinout,
actual voltages, reset timing and both output-enable paths with each supply
and jumper independently present/absent, slow ramps, short dips and brownouts.
MCP100 output below 1 V, AHCT behavior outside its supply range, resistor and
leakage tolerances, power-good transient response, open/shorted wires, and
connector output loading are not certified by the nominal truth table.
Check MCU-to-AHCT and inter-ESP UART back-powering with either ESP unpowered;
neither supply ORing nor isolation is provided. Confirm no damaging current
or partially powered operation via USB, GPIO, sense or protection diodes.
Check relay/mute polarity and startup state against the real equipment.
The experimental routed candidates have native schematic-parity reports,
but fitted part/footprint correctness, power decoupling loops, clearances,
ground returns, EMC, inrush, RF/antenna and mechanical fit are not validated.
Independent schematic review and measured testing are mandatory before use.
