Pin reservation review — conditional, no remap
-----------------------------------------------
The exact board schematic, header assignments and USB-UART bridge wiring
remain unverified. For an Espressif DevKitC-1 reference with octal PSRAM,
GPIO35/36/37 are reserved for flash/PSRAM, GPIO19/20 for native USB, GPIO0,
3, 45 and 46 are strapping pins, and GPIO43/44 are normally UART0. The v1.1
reference uses GPIO38 for RGB; other revisions may use GPIO48. These are
reference cautions, not proof of this carrier's wiring or module internals.
Reference documentation:
https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.1.html
https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/gpio.html

The saved v5 netlist uses controller GPIO1,2,4,5,7,8,13,14,15,16,18,
21,39,40,42,47 and audio GPIO1,2,4,5,6,7,8,9,11,12,13,14,21,40,41,
42,47. None is in the above reference reservation list; this is not a
variant-specific confirmation. Spare buffered inputs are included. The
controller GPIO21/47 and audio GPIO13/14 are inter-ESP UART; audio GPIO8
is the local U2 enable. See Electrical-review-v5.txt for the existing map.
No firmware or GPIO mapping was changed. Verify actual header labels and
schematic, LED, BOOT/RESET, USB-UART TX/RX and N16R8 PSRAM wiring before
relying on these assignments. In particular do not infer an LED or UART
pin solely from the family name. Confirm J1.1/J1.2 3V3, J1.21 5V and
J1.22/J3.1 GND on the actual board as well; do not tie the ESP 3V3 rails.

Minimum information needed to unblock an actual CAD revision
------------------------------------------------------------
Prefer an exact carrier seller link/SKU and revision-specific dimensioned
drawing, schematic/pinout and module RF integration guide. Otherwise measure
both actual boards with calipers, power disconnected, using J1.1 pin center
as origin, +y along the J1 row toward USB and +x toward J3:

1. Pin count, center-to-center pitch along each row and J1-to-J3 row-center
   spacing; first/last pin offsets to each carrier edge, length, width and
   thickness, including tolerances and any nonrectangular protrusions.
2. Bounds of the complete module, PCB antenna and antenna connector relative
   to J1.1, including projection beyond the carrier; heights above/below the
   carrier, header/socket height and underside components. Identify the
   antenna's radiator and its vendor-required 3D substrate/copper/metal
   clearance, not merely the module outline.
3. Both USB-C receptacle positions and extents, their insertion directions,
   plug/cable widths, depth and bend clearance. Include the pigtail connector,
   bend and whip routing/envelope, and whether PCB or external RF path is
   selected. Obtain the manufacturer's selector/matching instructions.
4. Chassis walls, hardware and connector/cable envelopes at both ESP
   locations, with assembly tolerances, so overhang and access can be checked.
5. A readable, straight-on photo of both header labels and both sides or an
   exact schematic, plus the manufacturer/revision and LED, BOOT, UART,
   PSRAM and 5V/3V3 power wiring. Continuity checks unpowered can supplement,
   but do not by themselves prove RF selection or dynamic power behavior.

With these inputs, check both rigid socket rows, substrate and all copper
layers, enclosure/USB access and real antenna keepout before deciding
placement and rerouting. Refill/save, DRC, parity, net/domain and solder
clearance audits and previews must then be rerun on the same saved board.
Bench power sequencing, return-current and RF behavior remain separate
validation steps; zero opens is not sign-off.
