DAR-1000ES v4 — draft layout, not approved for fabrication or power-on
===================================================================

Open DAR-1000ES-v4.kicad_pro in KiCad 10. The PCB, three schematic sheets,
custom carrier libraries and library tables belong together. KiCad's
standard 10.x symbol and footprint libraries are also required.

Routing status
--------------
This is a partially routed draft. KiCad 10.0.2 reports zero rule violations
and 67 unconnected items with all depicted wires installed. Do not order,
assemble or power this design as delivered. Single-layer routing without
bridges and several constrained bridge-routing orders did not close the
design; remaining airwires need placement/channel and route iteration.

The saved draft has 1,399 B.Cu segments, no F.Cu tracks, no routing vias
and 36 board-only JW links at 2.75–14.50 mm pitch. Each link has two
plated 0.80 mm holes and one straight Y-axis insulated wire on top.
Normal through-hole annuli are present on both faces. Wire-schedule.txt
gives top-view coordinates; pitch is centre-to-centre, not cut length.
Opening all JW links without removing the holes or copper increases the
native unconnected count to 103. These links are not JP1/JP2 shunts.

The PDFs are review drawings fitted to the page, not 1:1 artwork.
The assembly sheet is top-view and the bottom copper sheet is mirrored.
No Gerbers or drills are packaged. Native DRC, ERC and omitted-wire DRC
are in reports/. The strict verifier reruns the checks and returns nonzero
while any open items remain. From this directory, with KiCad 10's Python,
CLI and standard libraries configured:

    /usr/bin/python3 verify.py --reports /tmp/dar-v4-check

Mechanical layout (top view)
----------------------------
The outline is X=0..100 mm, Y=0..300 mm. The six 3.4 mm unplated original
holes remain at X=5 and 95 mm, for each of Y=20, 110 and 200 mm. No extension
support is specified: check deflection under connector insertion and agree
chassis standoff positions before release. This is not a Sony chassis drawing.

The controller socket's antenna faces left and its USB end faces right.
The audio socket's antenna faces right and its USB end faces left. Check
the installed modules, USB plugs and enclosure around both ends; a socket
courtyard is not a validated antenna keepout or a plug insertion envelope.
U3/U4, U2 and the TCA8418 keypad carrier serve the left-hand cable bank.
U6/U7 serve the right-hand audio cable bank. Local power and Sony sense are
at the lower right. J8 exits at the lower edge beside the left-hand bank.
All cable connectors are horizontal JST-XH with the original pin numbering.

Use two ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8 modules, each with two 1x22 P2.54 mm
sockets on 22.86 mm centres. Use SN74AHCT125N and SN74AHCT00N PDIP-14
parts on 7.62 mm row spacing. U5 is Adafruit TCA8418 product 4918, with
two 13-pin rows at 2.54 mm pitch, 12.70 mm row spacing and a
35.56 x 17.78 mm module envelope. Verify seated height and orientation.

Wiring and supply review
------------------------
GPIO and SN74 gate delta from v2/v3: none. Use the direct map in
../baseline-v3/GPIO-and-interlock-v3.txt; do not assume firmware already
implements it. GPIO19/20 (native USB), GPIO35/36/37 (octal PSRAM),
GPIO48 (DevKitC-1 v1.0 RGB LED), GPIO38 (v1.1 RGB LED) and GPIO43/44
(USB-UART) are not assigned new functions. See the Espressif DevKitC-1
v1.0 and v1.1 user guides (including the board schematics):
https://documentation.espressif.com/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.0.html
https://documentation.espressif.com/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.1.html

The 100k pulldowns default the five active-high ENABLE inputs LOW. The
AHCT00 gates assert /OE LOW only when ENABLE and SONY_POWER_GOOD are both
HIGH; the /OE pullups connect to LOCAL_5V, not to ESP outputs. Use the
specified MCP100-475DI/TO package/pinout and verify its flat-side orientation.
J12 is Sony 5 V sense only, not a power input; the two supplies share GND.

C9: 470 uF, 16 V Panasonic EEU-FR1C471 (D8 x 11.5 mm, pitch 3.5 mm),
    LOCAL_5V input reservoir.
C10/C12: 100 uF, 16 V Panasonic EEU-FR1C101 (D5 x 11 mm, pitch 2 mm).
C11/C13: 100 nF ceramic (D5 x W2.5 mm, pitch 5 mm).
C10/C11 are on ESP_CTRL_5V after JP1; C12/C13 are on ESP_AUDIO_5V after
JP2. Electrolytic pin 1 is positive; pin 2 is GND. JP1/JP2 are removable
supply shunts, not permanent wire links. Check USB/external power
isolation, inrush, leakage/back-powering, Sony brownout and both supply
orders on the bench. The interlock is not supply ORing.

Outstanding electrical review
-----------------------------
The schematic still has 63 inherited ERC review items: 47 pin_not_connected,
10 power_pin_not_driven, 2 pin_not_driven, 3 label_dangling, and
1 same_local_global_label. None is waived. FLSTB/FLSDATA/FLSCLK-to-J4
mapping remains unresolved; no mapping was inferred. Verify actual
Sony harness wiring, passive values, return paths, timing, RF and audio
performance before applying power. No real-hardware testing has been done.
